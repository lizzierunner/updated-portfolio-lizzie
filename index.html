Case 01 · Workshops 03–05 · After Effects

Weezer and the Red Planet

A vintage sci-fi trailer for a giant alien dachshund who only wants his ball.

The Brief — A funny vintage sci-fi movie poster starring my dachshund, Weezer, as a giant alien life form on a distant red planet. Humanity spends centuries searching the galaxy for intelligent life — only to discover the mysterious creature just wants a ball. Inspired by over-the-top 1950s–60s sci-fi posters: giant monsters, dramatic type, bright colors, mixed with Weezer's personality. The composite was built in Photoshop (Weezer cut out and dropped into an AI-generated cosmic scene); the red planet, rocket, UFO, and title type are vector art from Illustrator.

Process — WS03 animated the poster into a cinematic trailer title card: title slam entrance, tagline fade-in, moving rocket and UFO, drifting planet, slow zoom on Weezer. WS04 added a 360° rocket rotation, planet rotation, UFO scale wobble, a subtle Weezer rotation for weightlessness, title bounce on entry, easy ease on every keyframe, and motion blur across all layers. WS05 added post polish: drop shadow on the title, a glow on the planet, and the planet layer set to Screen blend mode.

Case 02 · Project 02 · After Effects

A Grand Dog Out

To the moon… for cheese. A Wallace & Gromit homage built from real desert photographs.

The Brief — A Wallace & Gromit homage where Weezer builds a rocket and heads moonward for cheese — told entirely with real photos I took at White Sands National Park. The goal: turn still photography into a narrative animation with a handmade, stop-motion-ish charm.

Process — Ken Burns pushes on the White Sands stills fake the camera movement, caption text pops in with a scale bounce for that storybook beat, and a grain overlay on Screen blend mode ties the real photos and the animation together. The rocket sequence was the ambitious bit. When my iMac's drive filled mid-project, I moved the .aep to a local Downloads folder so iCloud would stop fighting me.

Case 03 · Workshop 06 · After Effects

The Weezer Files

A hard-boiled dachshund gumshoe takes the case in a city full of shadows.

The Brief — A film-noir detective short starring Weezer as a very small, very determined gumshoe. A mood-first piece — high-contrast lighting, venetian-blind shadows, hard-boiled title cards — built to push tone and pacing in After Effects.

Process — Classic noir contrast: deep blacks, a single hard key light, and slatted shadow overlays for that "someone's watching" feel. Timing stays slow and deliberate so each beat lands.

Case 04 · Workshop 07 · After Effects

Run For Your Life

Zombies behind you, one mile to go — a kinetic-typography sprint through the Zombies, Run! world.

The Brief — A kinetic-typography motion graphic inspired by Zombies, Run! — the app that's gotten me through more miles than I'll admit. The brief: build urgency and momentum through type, pace, and visual rhythm alone.

Process — Everything serves the chase — text accelerates, cuts tighten, and the composition never quite lets you rest. Exported as an H.264 .mp4.
